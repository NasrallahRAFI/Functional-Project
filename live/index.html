About this readout
Drone/no-drone labels use a provisional −3.00 raw-logit threshold and a two-of-three window majority. The threshold comes from one 9.4-minute ambient-only recording; false-alarm performance is not established, and validation negatives often crossed it. Labels are provisional. Quiet input is standby.

Local by design
Audio is captured through this computer’s microphone and processed by its local Python service. Sound from a phone or speakers must be audible to that microphone; headphone audio is not captured.
