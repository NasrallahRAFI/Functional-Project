## V3-004 Channel recipe and label-safety boundary

Every training example independently receives either the V2 crop/±12 dB gain branch (probability 0.20) or the channel branch (probability 0.80), independent of class. The channel branch uses the fixed plan ranges: speaker HP 150–600 Hz/LP 3.5–7.5 kHz with probability 0.7; smooth EQ ±6 dB with probability 0.5; soft clipping 0–18 dB drive with probability 0.3; gain −30 to +6 dB with probability 1; synthetic room RT60 0.2–1.0 s/DRR −5 to +10 dB with probability 0.5; added background SNR 0–30 dB with probability 0.6; and an absolute −75 to −45 dBFS microphone floor with probability 1. Initial optional separate ablations: DC and hard clipping, exact codec/compressor parameters, pitch/time stretch and SpecAugment. Optional stages do not enter by implication.

Apply channel effects before crop/frontend, with explicit context and boundary checks. SNR is measured on the scored crop against the sum of added noise and floor. Do not alter or relabel low-SNR originals. Training donors are eligible non-silent training negatives only, with DC removal and donor provenance. Held-out augmentation uses fixed procedural noise/room seeds and never training audio donors.

Before any fit, freeze the exact implementation/parameter file after training-only energy, frequency-response, DRR/SNR and crop-boundary checks. Listen to 20 augmented training positives and 20 negatives and inspect spectrograms and measured levels. The current ranges are planning bounds; calibrations may be recorded before A0/A1, without inspecting canary outcomes.

**Training-only spectral survival guard (fixed before calibration):** after dividing each clip’s mel energies by its own total mel energy, the severe-transform positive set must have at least 90% of examples retaining ≥1% of their clean normalized energy in the model’s 50–7,500 Hz mel bands, and its fifth-percentile retained fraction must be ≥1%. This guard checks that the selected filter/noise recipe does not erase almost all model-band energy across positives. If it fails, narrow only the severe filter/SNR bound on training data, record the revised range before rendering the frozen validation suite, and rerun this guard. It does not guarantee class recognizability.

## V3-006 Threshold and eligibility rule

For each checkpoint, choose the raw-logit threshold maximizing weighted pooled F1 across the clean/augmented validation suite; ties choose the larger threshold. This explicitly extends D044’s max-F1/tie-to-higher rule from clean validation to the preregistered weighted pool. Freeze one threshold across every condition and live replay. Report clean-only max-F1 and validation-derived 1%/5% FAR cutoffs as secondary comparisons only.

A candidate is eligible only when the chosen threshold yields: (a) clean validation recall ≥159/185; (b) clean validation false positives ≤5/1,325; (c) each augmented-condition FAR ≤1%; (d) mean recall over the three augmented conditions at least five percentage points above frozen V2 on the same conditions, with no severity more than three points below V2. Rank eligible finalists by mean weighted pooled F1 across seeds 42/43/44, requiring seed 42 also to pass every guard. Scores within 0.005 pooled F1 prefer fewer optional transform groups, then lower measured runtime cost, then earlier preregistered configuration order. If none qualify, retain V2.

Final clean-test acceptance is at least 154/180 recall with at most 5/1,326 false positives. It is a report gate only, never a selection rule. A single frozen finalist enters one locked V3 job for clean and the three already frozen augmented test conditions. Preserve V1/V2 locks, disclose test reuse, and use paired group-bootstrap samples with all conditions of each original group kept together. Do not tune after opening V3 test outcomes.
