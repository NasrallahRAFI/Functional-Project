# CNN V2.0 debugging, testing and tuning plan

The question is why a Bebop video reportedly produced a brief detection followed by a sustained negative result. Keep the frozen depth-4 CNN as the reference. The first experiment should locate where the signal changes between the original soundtrack, the sound reaching the laptop microphone, and the decision shown in the browser.

## Evidence and limits

- The deployed model identity, normalization, threshold and 16/44.1/48 kHz software parity were verified. That comparison starts with digital samples and cannot measure Windows, microphone firmware, speaker acoustics or the room.
- All 80 windows from the four saved WAVs scored below the frozen raw-logit threshold of 2.82396. The highest, −0.905, was also below both alternative validation cutoffs, 1.894 and −0.221. The benchmark drone-class fifth percentile was 0.099. The saved clips lack confirmed event labels, playback timestamps and the live session's preceding vote history.
- The four recordings averaged −45 to −47 dBFS over ten seconds. Raw training drone clips had a median near −25 dBFS, with training gain sampled uniformly within ±12 dB. Individual training examples span a wider range. Adding up to 18 dB to the recordings still produced zero crossings at the frozen threshold. Digital gain changes signal and noise together. Drone audibility and signal-to-noise ratio remain open questions.
- Validation and test drone recall were 88.6% and 88.3% on the project corpus. The model selected at epoch 23 had training loss 0.091 and validation loss 0.254. Early stopping and the similar in-corpus recalls make the loss gap alone insufficient evidence that memorization caused this incident. Coverage is narrow, with two drone types, random crops and gain augmentation, and a reused V1 benchmark. The training loop lacks dedicated playback-channel, room-response and noise-mixing augmentation.

## 1. Reproduce the event with the frozen software

The initial saved-file replay is complete. Starting a fresh session for each WAV produced 80 scored windows, zero quiet windows, eight warm-up outputs and 72 final negative outputs. Individual window RMS ranged from −48.44 to −38.40 dBFS. The highest logit was −0.90548. The local CSV at `runtime/diagnostics/cnn_v2_saved_window_trace.csv` contains the audio offsets, gate, scores and votes. Time zero is the start of each saved WAV. A live session can enter that interval with earlier votes and a different window alignment.

### Digital validation control completed

The first labeled Bebop validation clip, `yes_drone/B_S2_D1_077-bebop_000_.wav` from group `B_S2_D1_blk077`, is 16 kHz mono and 1.024 seconds long. Repeating its samples continuously for eight seconds through the same `LiveState` used by the HTTP service produced 16 windows. Raw logits ranged from 8.002 to 9.809, all above the frozen 2.82396 threshold. After the two-window warm-up, every final decision was drone. This confirms that this validation source is detected by the direct digital streaming path. Repeating a short clip introduces loop boundaries and does not test phone playback, Windows capture or the suspect video. The per-window trace is at `runtime/diagnostics/cnn_v2_validation_bebop_trace.csv`.

1. Record the Bebop video identifier, exact playback interval, when the drone is audible, and the timestamp of the brief UI detection. Capture a little background before playback and several seconds after. Use one continuous session. Record speaker, volume, laptop input device, distance, orientation, room and model identity. Label uncertain intervals as unknown.
2. First repeat the original setup. Then set the phone to a reproducible starting volume around 60%. At that fixed volume and distance, compare Windows microphone enhancements enabled versus disabled where supported. Repeat each condition at least three times as a practical starting point. Change volume in a separate comparison. Include the same setup with playback stopped. Use a continuous, steady portion of the source to distinguish a level change in the input from a change in the video itself.
3. Capture the browser microphone track's actual settings with track.getSettings(), including sample rate, echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain control where exposed. The browser already requests all three processing options off. Record Windows and device-driver enhancement settings separately. Reopen capture after changing settings. A missing browser property means unknown.
4. Add an optional local trace at the point where the service iterates over individual windows. It currently returns the last result from each request. Log audio start/end sample and time, playback offset, generation, sequence gaps and resets, RMS, peak, clipping, quiet flag, raw logit if scored, raw vote, three-vote history, final state and UI timestamp. Align via a recorded playback-start marker. Store private audio and traces locally.
5. Inspect onset, steady playback and offset. The window and cadence are 0.496 s. All-zero audio or window RMS below −80 dBFS triggers standby and clears votes. A whole-file RMS of −47 dBFS cannot decide whether a particular window gated. After a reset the first two scored windows warm up the smoother. A drone decision requires two positive votes among the last three. One isolated crossing with two negatives cannot create a positive display. Two adjacent crossings can keep the display positive across two decisions, about one second.

**Decision** If steady source audio fades in the captured WAV and the effect repeats only with enhancements enabled, pursue capture configuration before changing weights. If a gap or quiet window clears the votes, investigate that interval. If the WAV stays audible and the raw scores stay low, proceed to the paired control. An untimestamped recollection of a flash cannot distinguish these outcomes.

## 2. Separate source, speaker and microphone effects

Use a labeled validation Bebop segment for repeatable development. The proposed test-split Bebop control is reserved for a documented, fixed demonstration using its existing frozen score if permitted under the course protocol. Use the validation clip for any comparison that will influence a setting, threshold or model choice. Loop the identical source long enough to fill three windows. Mark loop boundaries and compare aligned interior windows.

| Input | Procedure | What the comparison answers |
|---|---|---|
| Direct digital Bebop file | Feed the loop as a continuous stream through the live builder. Save the time series at the frozen threshold. | Does the source itself sustain detection under the live decision rule? |
| Same loop on phone speaker | Record through the browser at the marked microphone position. | How much does this playback and capture path change scores versus direct input? |
| Same loop on PC speakers | Repeat at the same position and approximately matched captured level. | Is degradation specific to one speaker or common to both paths? |
| Exact suspect video excerpt | Compare its direct audio when available, then its phone and PC replays with the same settings. | Is the original soundtrack already difficult, or do scores fall on acoustic replay? |
| Matched negative controls | Record room background and non-drone audio played through each speaker. | Can a setting that improves drone detection also produce false alarms on playback? |

If the digital control fails, check alignment, the loop boundary and clip scores before interpreting either speaker. If digital passes and both acoustic controls repeatedly fail, investigate captured spectrum, signal-to-noise ratio and enhancements. If only one path fails, isolate that device or placement. A passing Bebop control supports that particular setup. Direct failure on the suspect soundtrack implicates source content or model coverage. Direct success followed by replay failure implicates the replay path for that source. If the source file is unavailable, leave those explanations unresolved.

## 3. Tune one CNN only if the controls justify it

1. Open a decision-log entry before the next versioned experiment. Keep the same architecture and frozen V2 reference. Specify the target condition, validation selection rule, acceptable clean-recall loss and false-alarm budget before fitting. Derive candidate operating points from validation using the project's D044 rule and freeze each threshold for robustness comparisons.
2. First use the existing training split only. Compare the current crop/gain recipe against speaker band-limiting, then mixing with non-drone backgrounds from training at several signal-to-noise ratios, then synthetic room reverberation. Apply transformations only to training examples for fitting. Measure each addition separately under the same seed, budget and stopping rule. Keep source groups and all derivatives within their original partition.
3. Use clean validation plus a prespecified, reproducible playback simulation on validation sources. Evaluate continuous streams through the actual gate and smoother. Report positive events detected, detection delay, fraction of audible time detected, quiet/reset counts and false alarm events per hour of negative audio, alongside clean clip recall and F1. Compare with frozen V2 on identical inputs. Advance a candidate only if it meets the predeclared recall and false-alarm criteria. Short demos establish reproducibility, not a precise hourly false-alarm rate.
4. If one-dataset augmentation remains inadequate, propose **CNN V3.0** and obtain owner approval in the project decision log before adding labeled real recordings as a second data source. Document provenance, labels, split policy, rationale and the effect on the course comparison. Keep complete sessions, source videos and their replays together. Reserve fresh independent sessions for one final evaluation after weights, threshold and decision policy are frozen. The four previously inspected files serve as debugging examples. Repeated use disqualifies them as independent final evidence.

## Outputs and completion criteria

The monitor now has a **Download session trace** action. It saves local JSON with one row per backend window, elapsed monitor time, source rate, raw logit and threshold vote, three-vote history, window RMS and quiet-gate status, browser capture settings, sequence, generation, drop and reset counters. It contains no microphone audio. Start monitoring before video playback and note the elapsed time when playback begins so the saved rows can be aligned with the video interval.

Deliver the event-aligned trace, paired playback results and a decision-log entry identifying the smallest supported change. Record unresolved factors explicitly. A capture fix needs a repeatable benefit under the same source and negative controls. A model change needs validation evidence against frozen V2 plus the agreed final evaluation. Retain the existing test result as historical evidence under the project's one-dataset and judge-once rules (PROJECT_CONTEXT_V8.md, sections 1, 4 and 10).
